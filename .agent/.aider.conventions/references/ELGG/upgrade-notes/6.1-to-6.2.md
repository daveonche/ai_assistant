# Plugin Upgrade Notes: 6.1 to 6.2

Delta guidance for updating Elgg plugins from 6.1 to 6.2, distilled from
<https://learn.elgg.org/en/stable/appendix/upgrade-notes/6.1-to-6.2.html>.
The transition is deprecation-only: the three legacy TheWire functions
are deprecated, and only `thewire_filter()` has a stated replacement,
`elgg_format_html()`.

## Deprecated APIs

- `thewire_save_post()`
- `thewire_get_hashtags()`
- `thewire_filter()` — use `elgg_format_html()` as a replacement
